# Sales numbers and financial provenance

sales_report uses canonical sales analytics. Sold revenue is accepted quote value. Realized revenue is completed-job price. Neither is cash collected, profit or net income. Missing costs remain unknown. finance_profit_loss uses canonical cash-basis accounting and requires an explicit permitted finance space and inclusive calendar dates. Never mix an employee’s private finance space with company accounting. Preserve source warnings, attribution and currency limitations. Source: sales-analytics.js, finance-accounting.js and mcp/registry.js.
