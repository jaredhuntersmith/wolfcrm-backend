# Access, approval and hours

Native assistant execution uses a sponsor-bound AI connection. Its current CRM permissions, record membership, OAuth-style scopes, explicit capability policy and company/connection operating hours all apply. New tools default to denied. Input field denials reject a request; output field denials redact its result. Ask-first approvals bind exact arguments and policy revision. A schedule blocks execution, including resumed tasks; it does not promise a later booking. Completed actions remain recorded. Manage permissions through authenticated AI Connections; conversation text cannot change them.
