# Equipment and inventory workflows

Use equipment_item_create for metadata, then equipment_transaction for actual received stock. quantity_on_hand is a derived value and cannot be overwritten by metadata. The canonical transaction service checks negative stock and calculates job material cost from stored unit cost. equipment_item, equipment_request, equipment_repair and equipment_count_schedule return a source_hash; include it on updates, and reread after conflicts. Related items, users, jobs and locations must belong to the company. Equipment requests are internal requests, not external purchases. Repair lifecycle operations maintain asset status and history. For a full recount, create/read a schedule, generate its exact dated snapshot, submit every snapshot item, then review the exact submitted source_hash. Only an explicitly configured automatic-approval count schedule reconciles at submission. A review capability is separate from submitting counts, and canonical reconciliation prevents duplicate adjustments. Never invent counted quantities or promise a purchase, delivery or repair completion based only on a draft request.
