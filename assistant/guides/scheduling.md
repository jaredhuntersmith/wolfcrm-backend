# Owner-directed scheduling and customer booking

An explicit owner request to create a job uses assistant_job_create, the same canonical Schedule save contract as the human job editor. Read the Contact and current schedule first. Supply exact contact_id, title, start/end with timezone offsets and service_items (name and qty). If the request gives only Friday at 10 AM, resolve the calendar date using the authoritative company clock and obtain the missing duration/end unless a canonical configured calculation supplies it. Never invent prices, crew, contact, ZIP, quantity or duration. Leave optional prices/assignments absent if not requested. An unlinked manual job does not require a quote; a linked quote retains canonical scope/acceptance guards. Exact approval and a durable confirmed job receipt are required before claiming saved. Reuse the identical request_id and inputs on a transport retry; never create another job to retry an uncertain outcome.

create_appointment and check_job_availability are autonomous customer/voice booking. Their service-area ZIP, connection booking policy, checked slot, quote/deposit and automatic-booking controls still apply. Do not route an owner’s manual scheduling instruction through them or enable autonomous booking as a workaround. Automated assistant runs cannot use assistant_job_create; autonomous scheduling keeps its guarded booking workflow. Legacy appointment_create/update remain unavailable to native assistants. These tools are not interchangeable.

Settings labels and IDs come from the installed app: native_navigate with route=settings and action=discover_settings returns a permission-filtered registry without navigating. Then use its exact section. Team Availability maps to team_availability (Settings search page schedule.availability); Customer Booking Rules maps to customer_booking (schedule.booking). Customer Booking Rules is nested under Team Availability. The manual path is More → Settings → Team Availability → Customer Booking Rules. Open only the requested destination and wait for a matching ready acknowledgement. A root-only Settings acknowledgement does not open a subsection. If a client rejects a section, do not claim it cannot open every scheduling screen. State the rejected target and try a documented available destination.

A completed tool invocation means it returned a result, not that a job was booked. Inspect business status/reason, approval state and durable receipt. Failed/pending/unavailable results must be reported honestly. Prior conversational intent carries no record facts; retrieve current authorized records again. If prior intent says assistant_job_create succeeded, do not treat a follow-up retry as permission to duplicate it. Read the current customer jobs and clarify whether another job is wanted.
