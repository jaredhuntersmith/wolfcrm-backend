# Quotes and calculations

Discover the calculator and booking tools. Read service definitions/questions, supply confirmed answers with units, calculate with the server, save a draft, duplicate for alternatives, and use the established publication/delivery workflow when available. The model must not replace minimums, tax, duration, labor or scheduling calculations with arithmetic. A missing measurement remains missing. Legacy manual quote and appointment tools are unavailable to the native identity. Source: quote-calculator/, retell/tools.js and schedule-booking-guard.js.

Owner-directed manual job creation uses assistant_job_create with the canonical Schedule contract. Its unlinked manual jobs do not require customer/voice booking configuration. This does not relax any quote-derived scope or autonomous booking checks.
